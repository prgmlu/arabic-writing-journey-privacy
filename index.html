Arabic Writing Journey Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Arabic Writing Journey is an app for learning to write the Arabic letters and short words. It does not require an account. Version 1.1.0 and later shows ads, as described below; the lessons, the game, and free practice all work offline, and every feature is free. Earlier versions show no ads.

Age Information

The app asks for a birth year so it can choose suitable advertising. The birth year and exact age are not saved or transmitted. The app stores only one result on your device: adult, 13 to 17, under 13, or declined. Every learning feature is available to everyone.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app stores your learning progress on your device: lesson stars, the number of lessons and games finished today, your practice streak, your best game scores, and the age result described above. This data stays on your device so the app can restore your progress. The developer does not receive it.

Pronunciation

Words and letter sounds are spoken by your device's own text-to-speech service. The app sends it only the Arabic text to speak. Your device's text-to-speech provider, usually Google, handles that text under its own policy.

Advertising

The Android app uses Google AdMob, which takes part in Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK program. Ads are protected according to your age group:

  • Adults: the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request current privacy choices before requesting an ad, and shows a Privacy choices link on the map when Google reports that one is required. Adults may choose to watch a rewarded video to continue a game; watching is always optional.
  • Ages 13 to 17: ads are requested as for users under the age of consent. They are not personalized, the advertising ID is not sent, and only general-audience ad content is allowed.
  • Under 13, or if you prefer not to give your age: ads are requested as child-directed (under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act). They are not personalized, the advertising ID is not sent, and only general-audience ad content is allowed. No consent form is shown.

Until you answer the age question, the app requests no ads. Rewarded videos are offered only to adults.

According to Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosure, the SDK may automatically collect and share an IP address (which may estimate general location), app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers. Google states that it uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention and encrypts it in transit. Google handles this data under its Privacy Policy and advertising policies.

Other Third Parties

If you install the app through Google Play, Google may process Play Store, install, crash, and device diagnostic data under Google's own policies. The app does not include a separate developer-operated analytics service.

Deleting Data

You can change the saved age result with the Your age link at the start of the map, and review Google's privacy choices there when available. You can delete all stored data by clearing the app storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Android also provides controls to reset or delete the device advertising ID.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Users under 13, and users who do not give an age, receive only child-directed ads, as described above.

Changes

This policy will be updated before a future release materially changes these practices.
