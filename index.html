Arabic Writing Journey Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Arabic Writing Journey is an app for learning to write the Arabic letters and short words. It does not require an account. Version 1.1.0 and later shows ads, as described below; the lessons and the games work offline, and every feature is free. Earlier versions show no ads.

Age Information

The app asks for a birth year so it can choose suitable advertising. The birth year and exact age are not saved or transmitted. The app stores only one result on your device: adult, 13 to 17, under 13, or declined. That result stays on your device. Every learning feature is available to everyone.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app stores your learning progress on your device: lesson stars, the number of lessons and games finished today, your practice streak, your best game scores, the interface language you picked, the age result described above, and a random identifier for usage statistics. This data stays on your device so the app can restore your progress. The developer does not receive it, apart from the usage statistics described below.

Usage Statistics

To learn how the app is used and improve it, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, on servers in the European Union: lessons started, finished and left, games played, which ads were shown, the settings you pick, the app and Android version, the app language, and your phone's region setting. Your age result is not sent. Each event carries a random identifier that the app creates; clearing the app's storage or reinstalling the app creates a new one.

This is not the advertising ID. The events contain no name, nothing you write or draw, and no location, and IP addresses are not stored. The statistics are used only to improve the app: they are not used for advertising, and they are not sold or shared with others. Events are kept for up to one year, then deleted. Nothing in them links them to your name or email, so the developer cannot pick out one person's events.

Pronunciation

Words, letter names and letter sounds play from recordings that come with the app, so playing them sends nothing. Any text without a recording is spoken by your device's own text-to-speech service instead: the app sends it only the Arabic text to speak, and your device's text-to-speech provider, usually Google, handles that text under its own policy.

Advertising

The Android app uses Google AdMob, which takes part in Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK program. Ads are protected according to your age group:

  • Adults: the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request current privacy choices before requesting an ad, and shows a Privacy choices link on the map when Google reports that one is required. Adults may choose to watch a rewarded video to continue a game or to open a locked lesson early; watching is always optional. Where Google has no ad to show and no consent form is required in your region (for example in Russia), an adult may see an ad from the Yandex Advertising Network instead.
  • Ages 13 to 17: ads are requested as for users under the age of consent. They are not personalized, the advertising ID is not sent, and only general-audience ad content is allowed.
  • Under 13, or if you prefer not to give your age: ads are requested as child-directed (under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act). They are not personalized, the advertising ID is not sent, and only general-audience ad content is allowed. No consent form is shown.

Until you answer the age question, the app requests no ads. Rewarded videos are offered only to adults.

According to Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosure, the SDK may automatically collect and share an IP address (which may estimate general location), app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers. Google states that it uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention and encrypts it in transit. Google handles this data under its Privacy Policy and advertising policies.

The Yandex Mobile Ads SDK is not part of the Families program, so the app starts it only for adults, and never for users under 18 or users who do not give an age. According to Yandex's Google Play data disclosure, the SDK collects and shares the device advertising ID for advertising, encrypted in transit, and collects no location unless an app enables it, which this app does not. Yandex handles this data under its Privacy Policy.

Other Third Parties

If you install the app through Google Play, Google may process Play Store, install, crash, and device diagnostic data under Google's own policies.

Deleting Data

You can change the saved age result with the Your age link at the start of the map, and review Google's privacy choices there when available. You can delete all stored data by clearing the app storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app; usage statistics already sent are deleted after one year, as described above. Android also provides controls to reset or delete the device advertising ID.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The usage statistics are the same for every age: a random identifier and app activity, used only to improve the app and never for advertising. Users under 13, and users who do not give an age, receive only child-directed ads, as described above.

Changes

This policy will be updated before a future release materially changes these practices.
