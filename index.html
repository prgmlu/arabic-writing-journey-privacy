Arabic Writing Journey Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Arabic Writing Journey is an app for learning to write the Arabic letters by tracing them. It works fully offline and does not require an account. It does not show ads and does not include analytics or tracking.

Data Collected

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage data. The app makes no network requests.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app stores your learning progress on your device: lesson stars, the number of lessons finished today, and your practice streak. This data stays on your device so the app can restore your progress. The developer does not receive it.

Other Third Parties

If you install the app through Google Play, Google may process Play Store, install, crash, and device diagnostic data under Google's own policies.

Deleting Data

You can delete all stored progress by clearing the app storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

Changes

This policy will be updated before a future release materially changes these practices.
